## Players
- 2-4 Players
  - Play can start with 2 people, and 4 players is a hard, non-negotiable cap
- no Delta Green experience required, Call of Cthulhu players should expect unlearning some habits
- Need a browser that works with [Owlbear Rodeo](https://www.owlbear.rodeo/)
- A recording device (such as gaming headset or in-built microphone) is **strongly** recommended
 
## Time Commitment
1 to 2 sessions of 4 hours per month

## Campaign Setting
Contemporary-ish, some time during the Global War on Terror, ca. 2001

## Themes
- Conspiracies
- Investigation of conspiracies
- Things Man Is Not Meant To Know
- How far will you go?
 
## Genre
Horror

## Style
One off, with the possibility to become an episodic campaign

## Tone
The X-Files or Fringe but entirely not for children.

## Content Rating
An MPAA, ESRB Adults Only (PEGI 18) rating. Violence and horror are at the core of Delta Green!

## The Game Rules
This is a Delta Green game. ### Characters
Delta Green prefers to recruit federal law enforcement officers, special operations military, and specialists in federal employ (in roughly that list of priorities).

The pre-made characters are good choices to play or can be inspirations if you want to create your own Agent.

## Safety and Consent
Details (including specific safety tools) will be part of Session 0, including Lines and Veils. If you want to prepare something, check the links below.

### X, N, O Cards (subject to change!)
This is a check-in tool during the live session, and allows players to stop and/or pause any given scene/description.

#### X in chat/voice
The scene ends, and we take a break. The session continues after the X'd scene.

#### N in chat/voice
The scene pauses, and we can take a break. The session can continue with an [[#O Card]] after the break, or ends/fades to black without an O Card.

#### O Card
The scene continues after everyone checked in with an O.

### Lines and Veils
This is where we "draw a line" or "draw a veil": Lines are non-negotiable no-go zones, Veils can come up, but the scene will fade to black during live sessions.

As an example, I've added my personal Lines and Veils (if you insist on a Line, you need to find another GM)

- harm to children
- sexual violence
- queerphobia

#### cassie's Veils
